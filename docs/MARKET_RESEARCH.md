## Problem hypothesis

Incoming exchange students have to piece their move together from scattered sources: immigration requirements, university housing, transit, phone plans, weather, events and informal group chats. Much of that information doesn't apply to their country or length of stay. And it's hard to meet the students arriving in the same semester before landing.

We want to help each student answer three questions:

1. What should I prepare before coming?
2. What can I do once I arrive?
3. Who else is here for my exchange?

## Target customer

- **Primary:** students from outside Canada coming to the University of Calgary on exchange for one or two semesters.
- **Secondary:** a volunteer or staff administrator who curates events and moderates the community.
- **Future:** other host universities. The data model already supports more than one.

## Existing alternatives

| Alternative | What it does well | Gap for an incoming exchange student |
|---|---|---|
| University international office web pages | Authoritative and complete | Spread across many pages, not personalised by country or stay length, and no community |
| Government immigration sites (IRCC) | The official source of truth | Generic. Students must work out which rules apply to them. |
| Social media and chat groups (Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram) | Fast, social, peer advice | Unmoderated, mixes cohorts and years, full of outdated info and rental scams, and hard to search |
| Event platforms (e.g. Eventbrite, Meetup) and city tourism sites | Large event catalogues | Not filtered for students or the exchange calendar, and no Rockies trip context |
| Generic "moving to Canada" blogs and apps | Broad tips | Not specific to Calgary or UCalgary, often out of date, sometimes commercial |

## Our differentiation

- **Personalised, honest checklist:** the required steps change with home country and stay length, every item cites its official source, and unreviewed countries get "needs confirmation" rather than a guess.
- **One place for preparation, discovery and community**, organised around the student's **semester cohort**.
- **Privacy by default:** members choose exactly what others see, and the login email is never shown.
- **Curated, not scraped:** an admin console keeps events, guides and ideas accurate.
- **Separates dated events from ongoing ideas**, so a ski day is never shown on an invented date.

## Pilot success measures (proposed)

These come from PRD §12. They're learning goals, not forecasts.

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Onboarding completion | ≥ 80% of signups finish the required fields |
| Preparation engagement | ≥ 60% open a preparation resource in their first week |
| Event engagement | ≥ 50% of arrived students open an event or organizer link within two weeks |
| Community participation | ≥ 40% create a post or reply |
| Perceived usefulness | ≥ 70% rate it 4 or 5 out of 5 |
